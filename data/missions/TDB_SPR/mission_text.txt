Location: Hyperspace outside the Indulge system jump point
The operation draws near. Once it begins, your fleet is ordered to proceed first to the Indulge system jump point, destroy the heretic defense fleet there, and open a route for the main force behind you.

Outside the viewport, navigation lights and ship engines illuminate the darkness of hyperspace. So far, everything is proceeding smoothly.

You cannot say why. Your side holds an obvious and overwhelming advantage in this raid, yet a vague unease lingers. You rub your brow and force the thought aside.

"May great Ludd protect us."

You repeat the words silently.

Every unit is at the highest state of readiness. A priest aboard one of the ships is delivering a pre-battle sermon from the rear.

—"These abominations from other planes have joined with vile AI to corrupt our world. Now our great Lord calls upon us to reduce them to ash! We shall become His sword and deliver judgment. Our great Lord will bless us; may our sanctified armor hold fast! For great Ludd!"
