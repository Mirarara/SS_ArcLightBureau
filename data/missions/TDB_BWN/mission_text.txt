Location: Unknown
Beyond the viewport, the sea of stars appears as distant and immediate as ever. Yet only hours ago the First Fleet was confirmed destroyed in orbit nearby. Their deaths cannot raise even a ripple in this vast universe.

This is the first major battle since the Empire was founded, and at this rate it may also be the last. With our fleet hopelessly outnumbered, High Command has decided to use the Second Fleet—and the entire planet—as bait, buying time for the Empire's final heir to escape under the cover of the remaining fleets.

The communicator has carried no intelligible sound for some time, yet the terrible screams from the First Fleet's final transmission still fill your mind. We endured so many hardships... the better life we hoped for had seemed almost within reach...

But as commander of the Third Fleet—the last combat-ready force and the Empire's final hope—you have no time to drown in personal grief.

—"Third Fleet, respond! Third Fleet, respond! This is Second Fleet. We have lost more than half our ships and the defensive line has broken. The planet is about to fall! Take the princess and leave at once. As long as she lives—"
The communicator erupts with a massive blast buried in electronic noise, mixed with the screams and groans of crew, before the signal cuts out. On the radar display, the green contacts of the Second Defense Fleet drift like leaves in a gale, about to be swallowed by a locust swarm of red contacts. Conviction cannot always change how a story ends. They will not hold much longer.

A shrill alarm suddenly reaches your ears, and your body tenses.
—"Report! This is the Apogee-class Sky Star. We have detected a pirate fleet approaching—and they have not switched off their transponder!"

Only after several deep breaths do you notice that cold sweat has soaked your back. The first shock and fear soon give way to a steady surge of anger.
—"All ships, heed my command. Warm up weapons and raise shields. They are only pirates—follow me and break through!"

You pause as another thought occurs to you, then revise the order:
—"No—this will not be that simple. Wipe out every last one of those scum."
