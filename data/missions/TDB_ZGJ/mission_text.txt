Location: Indulge system
Repeated clashes have finally driven tensions between the Traverser Association and the Luddic Church to breaking point. Intelligence recently confirmed that the Church is about to launch its "Judgment Day" raid.

Yesterday we received word that the outer-system defense fleet had been attacked by a Luddic Church raiding force. Though not yet officially confirmed, the returning data indicates an invasion fleet of unprecedented size. The brave outer patrol fleet is almost certainly lost.

To gain even the smallest chance in the coming battle, the Bureau has authorized emergency deployment of the Cumulonimbus-class aviation fortress—even though she has yet to complete the inspections required for commissioning.

Navigation lights flash among the stars beyond the viewport while reports clamor across the bridge—constant reminders that a great battle is about to begin.

Your temples throb as you stare through the viewport. The stars seem to gather and merge into a single bright point, which suddenly blazes like a sun and sears your eyes.

"Headquarters reports that the automated fleet and three other fleets have successfully intercepted part of the enemy main force. We have a chance!"

—Your adjutant dims the 'star', and you realize the bright point was merely the maneuvering plume of a nearby escort.

Your adjutant sounds elated; few had believed the Skydome Bastion project could succeed. You scan the follow-up report from headquarters. The automated fleet and three other forces have cut off part of the Luddic Church's main fleet, but the force that broke into the system is larger than expected. If you cannot destroy every intruder, the encircled main force outside will collapse—and the Traverser Association will have no forces left to call upon...
There is no other choice.

A deafening alarm cuts across the bridge conversation as the radar blooms with anomalous high-energy readings from unknown sources.

"Enemy forces have entered the battlespace and are closing fast!"

"All ships: engines to combat power. Fighter wings cleared to launch. Prepare vertical-launch missiles; warm up ballistic and energy weapons!"
